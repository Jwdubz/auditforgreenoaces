What your site is costing you — Green O’Aces —

I put together an audit of the tweaks I'd make, plus a quick look at how your business shows up when someone searches for what you do. I went through greenoaces.com the way a Las Vegas homeowner planning a pool or a backyard would: search, open the site, look at the work, then call. The foundation is solid: your homepage loads with no errors or sideways scrolling, your address, hours and (702) 916-4777 are on every page I opened, your Contact page has a tap-to-call link and two forms, and robots.txt blocks only /wp-admin/. The leaks are in the details: from my connection most of the pages your menu links to sent me to a different website, your phone number can only be tapped on the Contact page, the homepage video downloads twice, and there is little on the pages to tell Google and AI tools who you are.

Checked October 23, 2026

What I found

01

From my connection, most of your linked pages sent me to a different website.

On October 23, 2026, I requested the 21 pages of your site that your homepage links to. Six loaded and one redirected to its own address with a slash added. The other 14 answered with a 302 redirect to freefoodvegas.com, which didn't load for me. That includes the pages behind SERVICES, GALLERY, INSIGHTS and PRICING TOOL in your menu, the “Read reviews” and “Visit our Gallery” buttons, plus your sitemap at /wp-sitemap.xml (the one robots.txt points to) and an address that doesn't exist. Chrome did the same for the four I opened in it. The redirect header says “x-redirect-by: WordPress”, so it comes from the site itself. Through a second connection, your Portfolio Gallery and Consumer Insights pages loaded normally. So it doesn't happen to everyone, and I can't tell from outside why it happens, who sees it, or whether Google does. It's worth having whoever manages the site check its redirect settings and plugins.

02

Your phone number can only be tapped on the Contact page.

Your homepage shows (702) 916-4777 twice, in the footer and in “Contact us or call (702) 916-4777 to schedule your complimentary consultation.”, and neither one is a link. On a phone-sized screen there is no call button in the header. Of the seven pages that loaded for me, only Contact Us has a tap-to-call link (tel:7029164777). On a phone, anyone who wants to call from the homepage has to copy the number or find the Contact page.

03

Your homepage video downloads twice, and on a phone its word is cut off.

On October 23, 2026, Lighthouse (a Google tool, simulating a Moto G Power phone on a slow connection) loaded your homepage at 22,547 KiB with a performance score of 71 out of 100. The main content finished at 16.1 seconds. The biggest files are your homepage video, vegas-pools-green-oaces-oasis-encode.mp4, which Lighthouse fetched twice (9,659,217 and 8,431,046 bytes), and two 2017 PNG images of about 2.3 MB and 1.9 MB. At 390 pixels wide the word “SOPHISTICATION” in the video shows as “HISTICAT”, and your headline, “LAS VEGAS CUSTOM POOL BUILDER & OUTDOOR LIVING DESIGN”, starts 1,958 pixels down the page, below the BizBot section. A real phone on a fast connection will do better than this test, and I can't tell what your visitors actually get.

04

There is little on your pages to tell Google and AI tools who you are.

None of the seven pages that loaded for me has business markup, the code that tells search engines and AI tools your business type, address, phone and hours. Five of the seven have no meta description: Pool and Spa Design, Outdoor Living Design, the award page and the two project pages. Both project pages use the same headline, a Tadao Ando quote beginning “The level of detail and craft”. The Outdoor Living Design headline reads “OUTDOOOR LIVING DESIGN”, and the award page's title is all lowercase, “las vegas best award-winning pools”. On the Fire and Water Features page 155 of 163 images have no alt text, and on the Outdoor Living Architecture page 62 of 68 have none.

05

Your Pool and Spa Design page jumps around while it loads.

Lighthouse measured a layout shift score of 0.521 on the Pool and Spa Design page. Google calls anything above 0.25 poor. That means text and images move after they first appear, which can make someone tap the wrong thing. The page also had a performance score of 64. This was one simulated run on October 23, 2026, and I can't tell how often real visitors see it.

What's working

The foundation is solid. Your homepage, Contact, Pool and Spa Design, Outdoor Living Design, award and two project pages load in a browser with no console errors on six of the seven (the Pool and Spa Design page logged three refused analytics requests) and no sideways scrolling at 1440 pixels wide, or at 390 on the three I checked at phone width. Your address, hours (Monday to Friday, 9 to 5) and (702) 916-4777 are on every one of them. Your Contact page has a tap-to-call link, an email link and two forms. Your homepage has its own title, description and a single headline, and robots.txt blocks only /wp-admin/. The link in your email signature, poolsandlandscapevegas.com, forwards to greenoaces.com.
